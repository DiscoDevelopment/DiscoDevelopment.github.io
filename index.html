Sam Disco


My name is Sam Disco, and I have a professional, personal, and pedantic interest in the American mental health crisis. I explore government policies and current events shaping the crisis, along with practical solutions we can build within our communities. My ongoing series, American Hive Mind, examines the tandem connection between hyper-connectivity and loneliness, and who is profiting from it.
